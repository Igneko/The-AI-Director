> *Nothing out there is random. Something watches every survivor and decides when to push, and when to let go.*

**The AI Director** turns Minecraft survival into a cooperative horror run. It is a pacing system inspired by the classic co-op zombie shooters: an invisible "Director" reads how the team is doing and decides when the world closes in and when it lets you breathe. Around it, the mod adds a full survival layer: a body that can break, teammates who have to pick you up, special infected that punish bad habits, and fortified shelters to aim for.

It is built to be played with **friends**, with guns, and with the lights low.

---

## 🎬 The Director

The Director is the heart of the mod. It never spawns a monster out of boredom: every ambush has a reason.

- **It reads the room.** A hidden *stress* value rises when you get hurt, when a teammate goes down (worse if they die), when you fight at close range, and when you make noise. It drops when you stay out of trouble.
- **It works in waves.** Tension builds, reaches a **peak** of chaos, fades, and then grants a real **calm** to heal, reload and share supplies. Learn to recognise the calm: it is the only moment you are allowed to relax.
- **It does not wait forever.** If a team plays too carefully and never peaks, the Director eventually forces a horde. Hiding is not a strategy.
- **It adapts the threat.** Calm brings silent stalkers; medium tension brings grabbers and chargers; high stress brings attackers that punish from afar. And very rarely, when nobody expects it, comes the **Behemoth**.
- **It respects shelters.** While the whole team is secured inside a safe room, the Director freezes.

It also takes over the hordes of **Enhanced Hordes**, deciding *when* and *where* they appear instead of leaving it to random timers, and it listens to the gunshots of **TaCZ**, so noise has a real cost.

## 🩸 Your body is a resource

Survival here is not about hearts. It is about how much damage you can afford before you need someone else.

- **Health that matters.** A hybrid health model replaces vanilla healing: wounds are real, and natural regeneration no longer fixes everything.
- **Incapacitation.** When you fall you are not dead: you are down, bleeding out, and you can only crawl and shoot a sidearm. Your teammates have seconds to reach you and pull you back up.
- **Black and white.** Get dragged down too many times and your next fall is permanent. Your vision drains of colour as a warning.
- **Medicine with consequences.** First aid kits, pain pills and adrenaline each have a role. Using them takes time, and time is something a horde will not give you.
- **The defibrillator.** A teammate that dies for real leaves a body behind. Reach it before it fades and the defibrillator brings them back: a few tense seconds standing still in the open.
- **Shove.** Pushing infected back saves lives, but it tires you out. Spamming it will not.

## 👥 Teamwork

Everything is tuned so that nobody survives alone.

- **Squad panel.** A compact HUD shows your teammates' health, state and distance. Allies are outlined through walls, so you can find a downed friend behind cover.
- **Audio cues.** You hear it when a teammate falls and when one dies, wherever you are.
- **Friendly fire matters.** Stats at the end of each stretch include it. Aim carefully.
- **Team-aware.** The squad respects your server's teams, so it works with minigames and custom setups.

## 🧟 The infected

The commons shuffle and swarm. They are rarely the real problem. The real problem is what the Director sends **between** them:

| Infected | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lurker** | A stalker that waits in silence and pounces on a lone survivor. |
| **Strangler** | Snares you from afar and drags you away. When it dies it leaves a choking cloud of smoke. |
| **Bloater** | A walking bomb of bile that attracts every infected nearby. |
| **Corroder** | Spits acid that eats the ground you stand on. |
| **Leech** | Leaps onto your back and does not let go. |
| **Rammer** | A charger that smashes through everything and everyone in front of it. |
| **Wailer** | A sobbing creature: startle her and she brings the horde. Rage is worse. |
| **Behemoth** | A towering brute that tears down walls and throws rocks. A real event. |

Each has its own sounds, its own behaviour and its own weak point. The in-game **Almanac** teaches all of it.

## 🔥 Arsenal

Guns come from **TaCZ**. The mod adds the rest of the toolbox:

- **Melee weapons.** Baseball bat, katana, fire axe, frying pan, crowbar and more, each with its own reach and rhythm. They deal their normal damage: a horde calls for spacing and timing, not for spam.
- **Pipe bomb.** Loud, attracts every infected in range, then explodes. Throw it far from you.
- **Bile jar.** Sends the horde after the target instead of after you.
- **Molotov.** A wall of fire that burns for a while. Special infected resist it; the Behemoth hates it.
- **Ammo piles.** Refill points that appear in shelters and never run dry.

## 🏚️ Safe rooms

A red door, walls that nothing can break, and a few minutes of peace.

Safe rooms are real structures generated in the world. Inside them nothing spawns, nothing explodes, and if the horde tears a block off the shelter **repairs itself**. Get the **whole living team** inside, close the doors, and after a few seconds the shelter secures: your checkpoint is saved, the Director rests, the downed get up and the dead **come back**.

Three designs, each with its own personality:

- **The Bunker:** buried in a mound in forests and hills. A zigzag trench, an armoured hatch, a long staircase, a decontamination airlock and a guard post.
- **The Hangar:** a corroded vault on open plains, with its huge gate welded shut by cargo containers. Mezzanine to shoot from, workshop, storage and a crane.
- **The Military Camp:** a trench of stakes, a high wall with a walkway and four watchtowers. Command post, tents, infirmary and a garden.

Shelters also hide **supply crates**. The Director fills each one *when you open it*, based on how the team is doing: if everyone is hurt, medicine; if you are healthy, throwables and ammunition. Everyone gets one share per crate. Some crates are **locked**, and only open after you survive a **panic event**.

### 🚨 Panic events

Some shelters hide an **alarm**. Pressing it means calling the horde on purpose: wave after wave, for as long as the siren runs. Survive it, and the locked crates in the area open. It is the most dangerous way to earn the best loot.

## 🗺️ Points of interest

Far rarer than shelters, and far bigger. These are not safe: nothing protects you here. In exchange, they hold the best loot outside a panic event, and more infected to guard it.

- **The Port Terminal.** A quay on the coast with five giant gantry cranes and a **200-block container ship** run aground. Explore its bridge, its holds and its engine room. Behind the quay lies a **maze of containers**: forced doors, hidden refuges, clandestine workshops and tripwires that fire arrows. Sniper rifles wait in the crane cabs, and the sealed hold keeps contraband weapons locked until you survive its alarm.
- **The Quarantine Dome.** A black ribbed pavilion between a motocross track and an asphalt circuit, hidden behind a dense forest with sniper nests. Inside: decontamination and triage, a workshop with armoured vehicles on hydraulic lifts, and a command centre with sealed research crates.
- **The Desert Forward Base.** A walled outpost on the dunes, ringed by a sand berm, gabion walls and concertina wire. Modular tents, a bombed-out concrete barracks with marksman nests, a 32-block radio tower and a hangar full of artillery.

## 📖 The Almanac

An in-game book that explains the mod from inside the fiction, in **eleven languages**. Chapters cover the Director, survival, teamwork, noise and stealth, the arsenal, shelters, points of interest and the full bestiary. It is the only manual you need.

## 🧩 Compatibility

| Mod | Role |
|---|---|
| **TaCZ** (required) | Gun system. The Director listens to its shots, and crates hand out its weapons and ammunition. |
| **Enhanced Hordes** (required) | Horde behaviour. The Director decides when and where it triggers. |
| **GeckoLib** (required) | Animated models for the infected. |
| **Player Animator** (optional) | Extra poses and animations. |

The mod is designed for **multiplayer** first and works on dedicated servers. Most of its rules can be adjusted in the configuration file: stress thresholds, healing rules, shelter behaviour, loot tiers, squad range and more.

---

## 🛠️ Building from source

The project is a standard **Forge 1.20.1** workspace using the official Mojang mappings. To build it, place the compiled `.jar` files of **TaCZ** and **Enhanced Hordes** in a `libs` folder at the root of the project, then import it into your IDE through its Gradle build file. Those two jars are used only to compile and are never redistributed.

Documentation: [Minecraft Forge Docs 1.20.1](https://docs.minecraftforge.net/en/1.20.1/gettingstarted/).
